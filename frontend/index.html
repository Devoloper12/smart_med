Empowering Patients and Peace of Mind for Families

Medication non-adherence is one of the leading causes of preventable medical complications. SmartMed bridges the communication gap between patients and their support system with real-time logging, compliance analytics, and caregiver alerts.

  • Adherence tracking with Chart.js analytics
  • MongoDB Atlas Cloud persistence for maximum reliability
  • Object-Oriented Domain architecture with inheritance
  • Separated deployment: Vercel (Frontend) & Render (Backend)
